Privacy Policy

Elite Four Pulls Store Manager is a private application used by the owner of the Elite Four Pulls eBay store. This policy explains how the application handles information obtained through eBay.

Information accessed

The application may access seller-authorized eBay account, listing, inventory, order, store, marketing, reputation, and performance analytics data. It is designed not to retain buyer personal information.

How information is used

Information is used only to audit store performance, prepare sales and inventory reports, identify listing problems, and recommend improvements to the owner's eBay store.

Sharing and sale of information

Information obtained from eBay is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or shared with unrelated third parties. It is used only for the operation and improvement of the Elite Four Pulls store.

Retention and deletion

Operational information is retained only for as long as needed to prepare reports and support the store-management workflow, then deleted when no longer required. Marketplace account-deletion notifications are received and acknowledged through the application's dedicated notification endpoint.

Security

Access is limited to the store owner and authorized application services. Reasonable technical safeguards are used to protect credentials and seller-authorized information.

Your choices

The store owner may revoke the application's eBay authorization at any time through eBay account settings. Requests concerning access, correction, or deletion may be sent through the Elite Four Pulls seller account.

Third-party services

Use of eBay is also governed by eBay's own terms and privacy notice. This application does not control eBay's independent data practices.

Contact

Privacy questions may be sent through the Elite Four Pulls seller account on eBay.

Effective October 12, 2026.
